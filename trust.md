# JJx10 Trust

*Aliens exist!*

**JJx10 Trust** is a 501(c)(3) non-profit irrevocable
trust to document and contextualize my paranormal
experiences from 2018-2026+. It is aimed at
explaining to the general public what the aliens
did and said to me, and sharing what they told me.

Its broader purpose is to work within the network
of extant disclosure or religious organizations
educating the general public about the esoteric
bases of these and similar experiences.

Donations are tax-exempt. Funds and assets are
only used for the purposes above, and should the
trust dissolve all property held will be
distributed to one or more similar 501(c)(3) 
organizations focused on disclosure or related
religious purposes. 

## Current Activity

* Scanning, redacting, and sharing the journals
that recorded paranormal experiences 2018-2026.
* Highlighting the material most in the public
interest, especially related to UFO disclosure
and esoteric/occult realities.
* Contextualizing the experiences within broader
collective knowledge about UFOs and non-human
intelligences. 
* Organizing and presenting unique revelations as a book.
